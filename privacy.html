Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information CALLYROSE may collect, why it is used, how it is protected and the choices and rights available to you.

Last updated: 24 November 2026
Important launch note. This is a working privacy notice based on the website features currently planned. Before collecting real customer information, CALLYROSE must add the legal operator's full name, business address and working privacy contact, confirm its actual processors and retention periods, and review this notice against its final operations. It is drafted with Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 in mind, where applicable.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

The business operating CALLYROSE is responsible for deciding how personal information collected through this website is used. The legal name and business address of that operator, together with a dedicated privacy contact email, will be published here once confirmed. We will not describe an unconfirmed email address as an official privacy contact.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number and account identifiers.
  • Order information: products selected, order history, delivery or collection details and customer-service messages.
  • Payment information: payment status, transaction references and limited payment details supplied by the payment provider. Full card numbers and security codes should be handled by the payment provider, not stored in the CALLYROSE customer profile.
  • Technical information: device, browser, log and security information needed to operate and protect the website.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, process orders, arrange delivery or collection, provide receipts and order updates, respond to questions, prevent fraud, maintain records, improve the website and meet legal obligations. We will only use information for purposes that are explained to you and permitted by law.

4. Lawful grounds for processing

Depending on the purpose, processing may be necessary to provide a service or take steps you request before a purchase, comply with a legal obligation, protect legitimate business and customer interests, or rely on your consent where consent is required. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it; this does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

5. Who may receive information?

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with service providers supporting the website, such as Supabase for account authentication and database services, website hosting providers, payment processors, delivery or collection partners, and professional or public authorities where legally required. The final list of providers and their roles will be confirmed before launch. We do not sell customer personal information.

6. International processing

Some service providers may process or store information in countries outside Nigeria. Where this happens, CALLYROSE will take the steps required by applicable data-protection law to ensure an appropriate level of protection and lawful transfer.

7. How long information is kept

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described, including account operation, order support, security, legal, tax and accounting requirements. The exact retention schedule will be set and published before live operations. When information is no longer needed, it will be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled as required by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong, unique password and keep your sign-in details private.

9. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, receive or transfer certain information, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where applicable. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use essential browser storage to keep you signed in, protect your account and remember necessary preferences. If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before they are used.

11. Children

The website is intended for people able to make purchases under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us using the published business contact details so we can review the matter.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website and services develop. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek consent for material changes.

13. Contact and complaints

For privacy requests or questions, use the official CALLYROSE privacy contact details once published on this page. Those details are intentionally not guessed or copied from a personal GitHub account. You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission through its official website at ndpc.gov.ng, where applicable.

14. Related terms

Read our Terms & Conditions for the rules governing use of the CALLYROSE website and orders.
